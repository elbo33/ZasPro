## Observations worth noting

**Lesson sizing.** Lessons are narrow. "Wartość bezwzględna" and "Równanie okręgu" are each one lesson covering one requirement. "Odczytywanie informacji z wykresu funkcji" is one lesson but covers a requirement worth 4 points on the exam. Sizing tracks exam weight more than curriculum text length.

**Chapter 7 is a catch-all.** "Funkcje i równania — pozostałe tematy" collects three topics that do not fit the other chapters. Worth deciding whether our tree wants such a bucket or distributes those elsewhere.

**Ordering departs from the podstawa.** Functions come before sequences, geometry is grouped late, and optimisation sits at the end as a synthesis chapter drawing on quadratics. That is a teaching sequence, not the regulation's domain ordering.

**Optimisation as a closing chapter** is a structural idea worth considering: a final section that reuses earlier material on applied problems rather than introducing new content.

**Practical-application lessons are separated** from their theory: lesson 12 splits practical uses of linear functions from lessons 9 to 11, and lesson 29 does the same for sequences. That is a deliberate choice about where applied problems belong.
